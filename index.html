About the game

Endless Crafting Defense combines real-time tower defense with resource collection and crafting. Place structures around the core, gather materials, and improve your defenses as enemy waves grow.

The game runs in your browser. Game progress is stored locally by the browser runtime on this device; this site does not provide account sign-in or cloud saves.
